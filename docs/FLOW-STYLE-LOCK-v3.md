*2026-05-20. Replaces v2. Reference: the Church's animated short "A Shower of Heavenly Blessings" (churchofjesuschrist.org). Generate on Nano Banana Pro. NEW Flow project — fresh start.*

## The target, named
**Cinematic painterly WORLDS + simple grounded CARTOON people.** Two layers, deliberately different — same as before — but the *character* idiom changes:
- We drifted too ANIME (big Ghibli eyes, delicate doll faces). 
- Target is grounded WESTERN CARTOON — closer to Pixar shapes / polished children's-book than anime. Friendly, simple, restrained. **Smaller, simpler eyes — NOT big anime eyes.**
- Backgrounds stay cinematic, atmospheric, painterly (this part we had right).

## THE LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (paste verbatim into EVERY character's Visual Description, after their unique line)

> RENDER STYLE (identical for every character): grounded 2D CARTOON in the style of a modern animated short film — friendly, warm, simple, slightly stylized but believable. Think polished Western animation / Pixar-shaped characters rendered in 2D, NOT anime, NOT Studio Ghibli, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D-rendered. Clean simple linework, flat color with soft gentle shading, smooth rounded forms. EYES: small-to-medium, SIMPLE cartoon eyes — a small round iris and pupil, gentle and warm, with at most a tiny soft highlight. NOT big anime eyes, NOT tiny black dots, NOT realistic human eyes. Faces are simple and rounded with soft features and minimal detail — readable from across a room. Natural, slightly stylized body proportions (a touch softened, not realistic, not exaggerated/chibi). Restrained, tasteful, dignified — a character you'd see in a heartfelt animated short, not a cartoon for small kids. Plain neutral cream background for the model sheet.

## Backgrounds (locations) — keep cinematic
> LOCATION STYLE: cinematic painterly background — atmospheric, with real depth, mood, and lighting (rain, snow, glow, reflections). Soft-painted but more rendered/detailed than the characters: buildings, streets, weather feel real and filmic. Background figures are simple soft silhouettes. The world is grounded and beautiful; the characters are simple cartoons placed within it. (This is the contrast that makes the short film look good — restrained characters in a rich world.)
